# Credits

## Sounds

The monster, player and ambience sound effects (`assets/sounds/monster/`,
`assets/sounds/player/`, `assets/sounds/ambiance/`) come from
[Freesound](https://freesound.org). They were trimmed, normalised and converted
to mono OGG for the game.

The water drips (`drip_*.wav`) and the ambient music
(`assets/sounds/music/dread_theme.ogg`) are generated by the scripts
`tools/make_sounds.py` and `tools/make_music.py`.

## Images and textures

- **Floor, walls, ceiling, damage, debris and signs**: drawn by the scripts
  `tools/make_textures.py` and `tools/make_signs.py`, with no external image.
- **Doors, locker, documents, note, keycard, battery and fuse**: images generated
  with AI (Gemini) for this project, prepared by `tools/import_textures.py`
  (originals in `assets/textures/source/`).

## 3D models

- **Monster, generator and exit door**: reference images generated with AI,
  turned into 3D models with Hyper3D, then converted to `.bam` with `gltf2bam`.
